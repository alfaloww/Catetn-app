# Catetn-app
CATET-in is a lightweight Point of Sale (POS) app built for small businesses to streamline cashier operations, manage inventory, and track daily sales effortlessly.

-Fast Checkout: Quick transaction handling with bold, high-contrast price readouts for accuracy.
-Custom Themes: 6 pastel color palettes to match your store's brand identity.
-Inventory Control: Simple catalog and stock management in one place.
-Sales Summaries: Clear daily transaction reports for accurate bookkeeping.

Ideal for: Cafes, retail shops, grocery stores, and local service providers.
